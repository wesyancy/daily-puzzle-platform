# Puzzle Research Notes

---

# Good Puzzles

## Example

COLD → WARM

### Why It Feels Good

- Strong directional intuition
- Recognizable intermediate words
- Elegant progression
- Feels solvable by reasoning
- Similar word structure
- Consonant/vowel positions align

---

# Bad Puzzles

## Example

STATE → PARTY

### Why It Feels Bad

- Feels directionless
- Too many branching possibilities
- Weak semantic connection
- Hard to intuit progress
- Structural mismatch
- Feels like random graph wandering

# Observed Weak Word Types

- Simple plurals may feel weak

- Generic nouns may feel slippery

- Visually similar words may blur together

- Strong conceptual words feel better

---

# Emerging Hypotheses

- Similar word structure improves puzzle quality
- Vowel-position alignment may matter
- Semantic relationships may matter
- Elegant puzzles feel “navigable”
- Bad puzzles feel “random”
